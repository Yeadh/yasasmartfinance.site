Who we are

Yasa Smart Finance ("we", "us") provides a family finance app and the website at yasasmartfinances.site. This policy explains what information we handle and the choices you have.

Information we handle

  • App records. The income, expense, budget, savings, loan and reminder entries you add to the app are your own. The app is designed to work offline and to keep these records on your device.
  • App lock. If you set a PIN or fingerprint lock, it is used only to protect access to the app on your device.
  • Website visits. Like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, to keep the site running and secure.
  • Messages you send us. If you contact us by email or through our Facebook Page, we keep the message and your contact details so we can reply.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the app and website.
  • To answer your questions and support requests.
  • To keep the website and app secure.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show advertising in the app.

Our Facebook Page and automation

We use a Meta (Facebook) developer app to publish posts to our own Facebook Page, "Yasa Smart Finances". This integration only publishes content that we create to our Page. It does not read, collect or store personal data of Facebook users. Access credentials for the Page are kept in secured, private storage and are not shared.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the website and Page (for example, hosting and Meta), when required by law, or with your consent.

Data retention and deletion

Records stored in the app on your device stay under your control and can be deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. To request deletion of any information we hold about you, such as emails you sent us, write to the address below and we will act on your request within a reasonable time.

Children

The app is intended for families and adults managing household finances. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.
